# AI Reasoning

**Tags:** #topic #ai-reasoning #optimization

## Overview

AI Reasoning encompasses computational approaches for solving complex problems through search, optimization, and decision-making algorithms. These methods draw inspiration from both biological evolution and mathematical optimization to find solutions in large, often discrete search spaces.

## Subfields

- **Evolutionary Computation** — Population-based metaheuristics (genetic algorithms, evolution strategies, genetic programming) that apply principles of variation, selection, and inheritance
- **Neuroevolution** — Combining neural networks with evolutionary optimization to evolve network weights, topologies, or both
- **Multi-Objective Optimization** — Techniques like NSGA-II for problems with competing objectives (Pareto-optimal solutions)
- **Population-Based Training** — Hyperparameter optimization through parallel population selection and mutation
- **Heuristic Search** — Problem-specific approximation algorithms when exact methods are intractable

## Key Concepts

- Fitness functions as objective measures
- Exploration vs exploitation trade-off
- Selection pressure and population diversity
- Genotype-phenotype mapping
- Convergence criteria and stagnation detection

## Relationships

AI Reasoning techniques are widely applied in [[Reinforcement Learning]] (policy optimization, architecture search), [[Machine Learning Foundations]] (hyperparameter tuning, feature selection), and [[Computer Programming]] (search algorithms, optimization problems).
