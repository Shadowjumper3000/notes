## Key Concepts

### Fundamental Principles
- **Vector spaces**: Sets with addition and scalar multiplication
- **Linear independence**: Vectors with no redundancy
- **Span**: All possible linear combinations
- **Basis**: Minimal spanning set

### Matrix Theory
- **Rank**: Dimension of column/row space
- **Determinant**: Scalar representing volume scaling
- **Inverse**: Undoing a transformation
- **Eigenvalues**: Scaling factors for eigenvectors

### Applications
- Computer graphics and animation
- Data science and machine learning
- Differential equations
- Optimization problems
- Quantum mechanics
